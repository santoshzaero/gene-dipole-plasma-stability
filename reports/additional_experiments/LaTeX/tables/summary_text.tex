\paragraph{Scope and runtime.} Across the six qualified samples, growth increases from 2.729 to 3.948 (44.7\%) and signed frequency from $+0.382$ to $+0.596$ (56.0\%). The available numerical controls change the fitted rates by up to about 0.20\% in growth and 0.034\% in frequency. The automatic spatial and velocity refinements also change the applied timestep. These changes are small compared with the contrast along the tested compensated path. The new integrations totaled 49.80 minutes of summed native wall time across 22 executions. Concurrent runs overlap in elapsed time. Exact inputs, checksums, full numerical diagnostics and additional figures accompany this report.
