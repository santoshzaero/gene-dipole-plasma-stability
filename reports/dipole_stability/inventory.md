# Research and figure inventory

The report groups the solver qualification, pair and isotope investigations, and numerical controls under Phase 1. The later common-gradient/wavenumber and electron-response experiments are Phase 2. The cleaned pair benchmark was recorded later but is grouped with the pair verification because it tests the earlier result. The chronology is in the [research log](../../docs/research_log.md).
