# Figure availability and exclusions

- **Electron-only and deuterium-only density-gradient curves.** No qualifying two-species IV records were found. With opposite unit charges and equal equilibrium densities, the derivative of charge balance enforces equality of the normalized gradients. Holding one fixed leaves only the common reference. The revised article implements one explicitly compensated electron-gradient path with kinetic helium at fixed deuterium gradient. The two-species gradient restriction still applies. The analogous deuterium-gradient path with compensating helium is designed but untested.
- **Low wavenumber.** The `kymin = 0.1` and 0.2 deuterium cases are unresolved. The anomalous 0.3 case has a GENE stop flag and 29 amplitude rescalings, and the raw complex field provides strong evidence against mixing it with the neighboring qualified result. The report shows the diagnostics and excludes those original rates from the common-gradient physics curves. Two half-step controls at 32 and 48 parallel nodes agree on a moderately growing mode at 0.3. The qualified 32-node result appears in the common-gradient physics curve at gradient 2. The exact unstable numerical term, low-wavenumber velocity-grid sensitivity and the global growth maximum remain open. [Fixed-step controls](../../docs/studies/deuterium_followup.md#half-step-and-parallel-resolution-results) The hydrogen samples below 0.5 are also unqualified.
- **Low drive and unresolved historical fits.** The five pair extensions and 13 isotope histories were recovered. Energy curves and window slopes were reconstructed. Frequency and a verified complex-mode continuation are unavailable from energy alone. Uncertain numerical estimates were kept in the table and omitted from validated rate maps. The older supplemental maps combined solver and estimated growth; the new report keeps those evidentiary levels explicit.
- **Phase 2 spatial plots.** Six selected local field binaries were offloaded or unavailable during the bounded raw-read attempts. Their already published complex samples were recovered from the repository and used where appropriate. The other selected profiles, including both electron-response cases, came from native fields. The trace source is recorded per case. Three offloaded duplicate local inputs are listed with blank metadata in the survey, and their corresponding tracked namelists remain accessible.
- **Theoretical dispersion and nonlinear behavior.** No independent ring-dipole dispersion calculation or nonlinear transport result was recovered. The electron-response result is a matched numerical comparison. The weak adiabatic rate still needs a targeted velocity-grid and timestep check.
- **Merged visuals.** Per-rate panels, duplicate heatmaps, repeated fit illustrations, convergence-cost views, and the intermediate stability map were consolidated. Underlying numerical values remain in the snapshot and original repository. The article favors comparisons that answer a distinct physical or numerical question.
